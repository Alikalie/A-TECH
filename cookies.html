Cookies Policy

Effective Date: July 26, 2026

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet or mobile) when you visit websites. They are widely used to make websites work, improve performance, and provide information to site owners.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for several reasons, including to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings (e.g., language, form inputs).
  • Provide and improve site functionality and performance.
  • Analyze site usage so we can improve content and navigation.
  • Enable third-party services integrated into our site (e.g., Jotform forms, analytics tools).

3. Types of cookies we use

Essential / Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the website to operate and cannot be switched off in our systems. They support basic functions like navigation, security, and access to secure areas.

Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies collect information about how visitors use our site (pages visited, time on page). We use this information to improve how our site works. Example tools: Google Analytics.

Functional Cookies

Functional cookies remember choices you make to provide a more personalized experience (e.g., saving form inputs, remembering a chosen language).

Targeting / Advertising Cookies

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies ourselves for third-party advertising, but third-party services embedded on our site (for example, social sharing or plugin providers) may set these cookies. These are governed by the third party's policies.

4. Third-party cookies

Third-party services that we use — such as Jotform for application forms, Google for analytics, or social plugins — may place cookies on your device. These services manage their own cookies and you should consult their privacy and cookie policies for more information.

5. Your cookie choices

You can control or delete cookies as you wish. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View the cookies stored on your device.
  • Delete cookies.
  • Block cookies in the browser settings (this may affect site functionality).

To manage cookies in common browsers, see the browser help pages. For example:

  • Chrome: Manage cookies in Chrome
  • Firefox: Manage cookies in Firefox
  • Edge: Manage cookies in Edge

6. Impact of disabling cookies

If you disable or block cookies, some parts of the website may not function correctly. For example, application forms may not save progress, and certain interactive features may not work as intended.

7. Changes to this Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted here with an updated effective date.
