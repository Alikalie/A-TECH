Privacy Policy for A-TECH COURSE

At A-TECH COURSE, we are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy.

Effective Date: July 25, 2026

At A-TECH COURSE, operated in Sierra Leone, we are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, share, and safeguard your personal information when you register for our ICT and Data Analysis programs through our website, Jotform forms, or mobile portal.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you fill out our application form or interact with our AI Assistant.

A. Personal Identification Data

  • Full name, date of birth, gender, and residential address.
  • Email address, primary phone number, and emergency/alternative contact details.

B. Educational & Academic Data

  • Highest level of education completed (Primary/NPSE, JSS/BECE, SSS/WASSCE, or Tertiary).
  • Last school attended, graduation year, and examination index numbers.

C. Identity Verification Documents (Uploads)

Depending on your academic status, we collect and store:

  • Recent passport-sized photograph.
  • Birth certificate or legal affidavit.
  • For WASSCE Applicants: WASSCE Result Slip and National Identification Number (NIN) / ID Card.
  • For BECE & NPSE Applicants: BECE or NPSE Result Slips and official Student ID Cards.
  • Recommendation letters from school authorities, employers, or community leaders.

D. Reference & Witness Information

  • Full name, phone number, relationship, and occupation of your designated guarantor or witness.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate educational and administrative purposes, including:

  • Application Evaluation: Verifying your academic eligibility and placing you in the appropriate course track (ICT Fundamentals, Data Analysis, or Combined Track).
  • Identity Verification: Confirming your identity through official state or school documents to prevent fraudulent admissions.
  • Communication: Sending confirmation emails, SMS notifications regarding admission status, class schedules, and updates.
  • Program Improvement: Analyzing demographic data to improve our curriculum and training facilities.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Encrypted Transmission: All form submissions and file uploads are processed over encrypted SSL/TLS connections.
  • Secure Cloud Storage: Submitted documents are stored in secure cloud environments integrated with Jotform, featuring strict access controls.
  • Access Control: Only authorized administrative and admissions staff at A-TECH COURSE can access your submitted application files.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We only share data with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our admission system:

  • Jotform: Processes form responses and securely stores uploaded verification documents.
  • AI Chatbot Service: Processes temporary user queries to provide dynamic help during form completion (no personal documents are stored in conversational AI history).

5. Data Retention

  • Successful Applicants: Student records and verification documents are retained for the duration of the course plus 2 years for certificate issuance and academic verification purposes.
  • Unsuccessful / Incomplete Applications: Document uploads and draft responses are purged from our secure servers within 12 months of the application window closing.

6. Your Rights

  • Right to Access: You may request a PDF copy of your submitted application and records at any time.
  • Right to Correction: You have the right to request updates or corrections to any inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request the deletion of your personal data if you choose to withdraw your application prior to course commencement.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact our Data Protection Team.

Cookie Policy for A-TECH COURSE

This policy explains how A-TECH COURSE uses cookies to support your website experience and registration journey.

Last Updated: July 25, 2026

At A-TECH COURSE, we believe in being clear and open about how we collect and use data related to you. This Cookie Policy applies to any A-TECH COURSE product or service that links to this policy or incorporates it by reference.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

Cookies allow our website to recognize your device, remember your preferences, and ensure that your online registration process is smooth and secure.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies on the A-TECH COURSE website and embedded Jotform application tools for the following purposes:

  • Essential Operations: Enabling you to fill out multi-page registration forms without losing your progress.
  • Security & Verification: Preventing fraudulent form submissions and keeping your uploaded document data secure.
  • Functionality & Preferences: Remembering your choices, such as language settings or dark/light theme preferences.
  • AI Chatbot Support: Allowing our A-TECH Assistant chatbot to remember your conversation context while helping you complete your application.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

The cookies used on our website fall into the following categories:

Cookie Category Purpose Essential?
Strictly Necessary Cookies Required for the website to function. They allow you to navigate the registration steps, upload documents (WASSCE, BECE, NPSE, IDs), and securely submit your application. Yes
Functional Cookies Used to recognize you when you return to our portal, saving your form draft or chatbot preferences. No
Performance & Analytics Cookies Help us understand how applicants interact with our landing page (e.g., page loading speeds, popular course tracks) so we can improve user experience. No

4. Third-Party Cookies

When you use our portal, third-party services integrated into our site may also place cookies on your device:

  • Jotform: Processes your registration form inputs, manages multi-step progress, and secures document uploads.
  • AI Chatbot Service: Maintains state for automated applicant support during your session.

We do not allow third-party cookies to track your personal browsing habits outside of the A-TECH COURSE ecosystem.

5. How to Manage and Disable Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject non-essential cookies.

Managing Browser Settings
You can set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies. If you choose to reject strictly necessary cookies, you may still use our website, though your access to certain functionalities (such as saving a form draft or using the AI Assistant) may be restricted.

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Block all cookies
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

6. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use or for operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Please re-visit this Cookie Policy regularly to stay informed about our use of cookies and related technologies.
