Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 26, 2026

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to A-TECH TRAINING ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, apply for our programs, enroll in courses, or interact with our digital services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies, relates to, or describes you ("Personal Information"). This includes:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Personal & Contact Details: Full name, gender, date of birth, residential address, phone/WhatsApp number, and email address.
  • Academic & Professional Information: Educational background (NPSE, BECE, WASSCE, Tertiary), exam index numbers, current employment status, and selected course tracks.
  • Verification Documents: Passport photographs, national ID/identification numbers, academic result slips, and recommendation letters.
  • Guarantor / Emergency Contact: Full name, contact number, and relationship to you.

B. Technical Data Collected Automatically

IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, pages visited, time spent on pages, and standard web log data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for legitimate educational and administrative purposes, including:

  • Enrollment & Academic Records: Processing your application, verifying credentials, and managing course enrollments.
  • Communication: Sending program updates, timetables, payment receipts, certificates, and important service notifications via Email or WhatsApp.
  • Student Support & AI Assistance: Providing prompt responses to inquiries through our automated support widgets or learning assistants.
  • Platform Security & Compliance: Preventing unauthorized access, fraudulent submissions, and ensuring compliance with applicable regulatory laws.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share information only under the following strict conditions:

  • Authorized Service Providers: Third-party partners (such as database hosting platforms like Jotform, email notification tools, or payment gateways) operating under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Regulatory & Legal Compliance: When required by law, court order, or official government requests in Sierra Leone.
  • Academic Verification: Confirming your certificates or educational records upon your explicit request or authorization.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. These measures include encrypted SSL/TLS website connections, secure database storage, and restricted staff access.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the educational purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law (such as keeping academic transcript records for certificate verification).

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Access & Request Copies: Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request corrections to any incomplete or inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: Request the erasure of your personal data, subject to legal and academic record-keeping exceptions.
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from non-essential promotional emails or announcements.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party platforms (e.g., WhatsApp, social media, or external educational tools). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party service you visit.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or relevant laws. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
